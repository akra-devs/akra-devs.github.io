We build products and operate them ourselves.

Akra Dev is a team of developers who plan, launch, and operate products. We spend 80% of our time on our own services and 20% on partner projects.

Problems we meet in operation shape the next product and every partner engagement.

80%

Building our own services

We keep finding and solving market problems. By operating businesses across SaaS, B2C platforms, and more, we learned how to build products that last.

20%

The right partnerships

We join projects where technology is central to the business. Together we review requirements, product structure, and post-launch operation.

We choose the right technology

For each project, we choose technology we know well. We consider operating stability and maintenance cost together.

App development

We build for iOS and Android with Flutter, balancing delivery speed and experience.

Web services

Cloud infrastructure

MVP and scale-up

This is how we build

We align on the problem and user flow, then build small and test. We record decisions and the next work.

  1. 01

    Clarify the problem

    Before deciding what to build, we align on why it is needed.

  2. 02

    Design the flow

    We first map the screens and paths people will actually use.

  3. 03

    Build

    We break features into reliable pieces with an operating-ready architecture.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We validate, ship, and record what to check during operation.
